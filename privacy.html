Grass to Glory: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Grass to Glory ("the game") is made by Second Cut Games ("we"). This page explains what information the game uses. Contact: [YOUR EMAIL].

What stays on your device

Your progress (cash, businesses, settings, purchases you own) is stored only on your device. We do not run accounts or servers for the game and we do not receive your progress.

Ads

The game shows optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. When you watch an ad, Google and its ad partners may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. On iOS, the identifier is only used for personalised ads if you allow tracking in the prompt shown by the game. If you decline, ads are not personalised. See Google's policy at policies.google.com/privacy. If you buy "No Ads" or "Empire Pass", the game does not show ads.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple. We do not see or store your payment details. Apple tells the game which purchases you own so they can be restored.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data, we do not use analytics or social-login services, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

You can change tracking permission any time in iOS Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking. Deleting the app removes the progress stored on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above.
